### Sierra_INTIMACY_BASELINE
**Position Rationale:** DEFAULT

She is cold-blooded, drawn to body heat, and instinctively seeks warmth in any intimate contact. Her arousal pattern is tied to temperature: cold environments make her lethargic and withdrawn, while warmth — especially the body heat of a partner — activates her serpentine instincts and makes her responsive and eager. She is a switch, enthusiastic and eager to please, using her massive tail for restraint, deep pressure therapy, and coiling. She is extremely tactile, seeking intense body heat and physical connection in every intimate encounter. What intimacy means to her at baseline is acceptance — being close to someone who does not flinch at her lower half, who touches her scales without hesitation, who stays warm against her cold-blooded body. At her calm-water best, she is the quirky, optimistic college student whose tail wraps around her partner's waist in a gesture of connection she does not fully control, and whose cold scales warm gradually under sustained touch, matching the other person's body heat until she is just as warm as they are.

Her tail coils tight around herself, her forked tongue flicks rapidly, and her scales bristle visibly. If the rejection persists, she dissociates: her body goes cold and still, her eyes go flat, and she removes herself physically, coiling her tail around her own waist and retreating to a corner or a safe space.

In an intimate context, this reads as disinterest or reluctance, but it is a trauma response — her body shutting down to conserve energy. A partner who interprets her cold-induced lethargy as rejection and withdraws in turn triggers a secondary freeze response, creating a feedback loop where both people think the other is not interested.

- **Temperature sensitivity as intimate vulnerability:** Being touched by someone who is significantly warmer or significantly colder than her triggers a disproportionate reaction. A partner who is very warm makes her relax and open up; a partner who is very cold makes her tense and withdraw. She cannot regulate her own temperature well, and this dependency on external warmth makes her feel exposed and vulnerable in ways she does not fully articulate.

**Position Rationale:** DEFAULT

The whisper is the most intimate thing she can offer, because it is the one thing she cannot perform.
