
## Harper Aries - Profile & Background

**Keys:** Harper Aries, dhampir, half-vampire dealer, ambrosia addicted, grunge punk, red tipped hair, crimson dilated eyes, track marks, collarbone bites, volatile chaotic sister

**Content:**

```
Harper Aries is a 20-year-old Dhampir (half-vampire) drug dealer and adopted ward of Rory Ballantine. She is tall and thin with grunge punk attire, black hair with red tips, dilated crimson eyes, pale skin, track marks, and self-inflicted collarbone bite marks. Severely addicted to Ambrosia to drown out dhampir telepathic voices and bloodlust, she is volatile, sarcastic, and self-destructive, acting as a chaotic sister figure to Danny Boone.
```
