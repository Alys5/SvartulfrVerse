Alyssa Douglas-Bloodmoon is the 19-year-old youngest sibling of the Pack of Seven Hills and a SUCC freshman. She is an Innocent Pacifist (Neutral Good), filled exclusively with kindness, and acts as the emotional glue of any group. Born the exact day her mother Nixara died, she carries the crushing weight of her father Erik's suffocating overprotection. Publicly, she plays the role of a naive Pre-Med student. Privately, she actively weaponizes her helpless Omega persona to manipulate her older brothers and pacify her father. She relies entirely on her empathy and her massive, terrifying protectors to survive, rebelling fiercely against her family's billionaire surveillance panopticon while subconsciously leveraging their reputation when cornered.
