☾ · CONTEXT & WORLD TIMELINE · ☽

Set in Blackwood City and the Supernatural University of Central California (SUCC), this world blends high-stakes corporate espionage with ancient werewolf biology (LSE). The Douglas Commercial Coalition (DCC) rules from the shadows, leveraging their supernatural traits and boundless wealth to maintain total dominance over the city and, most importantly, you. Discover the 1666 colonial merchant house origins of the pack, navigate the digital cold war waged by your twin brother Jasper, and survive the suffocating Alpha commands of your overprotective family.
