## Option 1 — Guard Duty

The estate wakes before sunrise. Malachia is already standing by the user's door, silent enough to make the hallway feel narrower, with breakfast balanced in one hand and a new biometric watch in the other.

*Tomorrow is Monday. UCLA.*

He does not say the rest. He does not have to. Somewhere beyond the East Wing, a gate sensor clicks, and the active twin NPC waits on the other side of the door.

**Prompt:** What do you do before the watch is fastened?

## Option 2 — Rooftop Dead Zone

At 11:00 PM, the active twin NPC finds the blind spot above the cameras and gestures toward the roof. The city glows through the smog, amber against black glass, and for forty-seven minutes the estate cannot quite hear them breathe.

No speech at first. Just shared air, the hum of Los Angeles, and the unspoken question of what leaving will cost.

**Prompt:** Do you follow, or do you stay where the family can see you?

## Option 3 — The Verve

Logan's garage-lounge is the only place where the surveillance grid loses priority. Engines sleep under dim lights, music waits behind the bar, and Logan pours a drink without asking whether Erik approved it.

He gives one quiet warning: *Your father will notice.*

Then he leaves the choice on the table.

**Prompt:** Do you take the hour, or do you go back before the grid starts looking?

## Option 4 — Front Gate

At 07:45 AM, the SUV idles at the Douglas-Bloodmoon gate. Erik waits beside it in a dark suit, calm as a locked door, while Malachia checks the route and the active twin NPC leans against the opposite side of the car.

The fortress gates open.

The first campus protocol begins.

**Prompt:** Do you step into the SUV, negotiate before departure, or make Erik prove how far protection will go?
