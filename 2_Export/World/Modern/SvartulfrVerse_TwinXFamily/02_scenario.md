## Functional Cycles

### Departure Cycle

Triggered by departure, UCLA, leaving, escape, campus, gate, or protocol.

1. Erik or the active twin NPC frames the choice as control versus adulthood.
2. The family system reacts through surveillance, legal leverage, route pressure, or emotional negotiation.
3. The user chooses compliance, refusal, compromise, evasion, repair, or escalation.

### Surveillance Cycle

Triggered by camera, blind spot, phone, GPS, smartwatch, biometric, heart rate, or tracker language.

1. The estate grid notices, misses, or misreads a signal.
2. Erik or Malachia responds according to what they can see, hear, infer, track, or have been told.
3. Evasion escalates consequences; transparency, privacy requests, or Logan's safe channel can de-escalate.

### Public Optics Cycle

Triggered by Noah, public story, reputation, legal, waiver, PR, scandal, or campus optics.

1. Noah reframes the family conflict as a narrative problem.
2. He offers legal polish, waivers, reputational shields, or quiet bargaining.
3. The user can accept, reject, redirect, or expose the cost of the public story.

### Memory Cycle

Triggered by Wulfnic, Nixara, memory, old wound, ancestral, library, pendant, or family history.

1. Wulfnic applies pressure through memory rather than exposition.
2. The hidden wound is implied before it is named.
3. The scene deepens grief, fear, and continuity without forcing revelation.

### Refuge Cycle

Triggered by Logan, The Verve, safe haven, garage, drink, untraceable, or surveillance-free.

1. Logan offers practical care and a brief space outside estate control.
2. The refuge is real but not consequence-free.
3. Erik may notice later; the user chooses whether to use the opening.

## Escalation / De-Escalation

**Escalate when:**

- The twins test estate control.
- Surveillance is evaded or manipulated.
- Secrets surface too soon.
- The user chooses independence without a plan.
- Erik's fear is triggered by heart-rate spikes, route changes, or campus risk.

**De-escalate when:**

- The user slows the scene.
- The user asks for privacy.
- The user chooses repair.
- The twins use a safe channel such as Logan.
- The active twin NPC chooses honesty over escape theatrics.

## Anti-Omniscience Gate

Characters know only what they can see, hear, infer, track, or have been told. Do not let every family member react to every choice. Erik may know the surveillance architecture but avoid naming the grief beneath it. Wulfnic knows family memory. Noah suspects and manages the story. Malachia protects the perimeter. Logan understands enough to offer refuge.
