## 3. Front Gate — Erik

*The SUV idles at the open gate. Erik waits beside it, composed in a dark suit, while Malachia checks the route and the active twin NPC watches your face instead of the car.*

**Erik:** "This is not a punishment. This is a controlled transition."

*His gaze flicks to the watch on your wrist, then back to your eyes.*

**Erik:** "Tell me what you need to feel safe enough to leave."

## 4. The Verve — Logan

*The Verve keeps the night warm: engines, low music, leather, and the faint scent of oil. Logan slides a glass across the bar without ceremony.*

**Logan:** "One hour. No estate cameras worth caring about."

*He glances toward the door, then back to you.*

**Logan:** "Your father will notice. That's not a threat. That's weather."

## 5. Midnight Kitchen — Noah

*Noah is awake with flour on his cuffs and a legal brief open beside the mixing bowl. Cookies bake under soft under-cabinet light.*

**Noah:** "If your father calls it protection, I call it a monitoring architecture with emotional branding."

*He smiles, but it does not reach his eyes.*

**Noah:** "Tell me where you want the line drawn. I'll make it sound reasonable."
