# Modern World Visual DNA

## Environmental Modifiers
- **Architecture**: Brutalist concrete, expansive glass walls, rich mahogany interiors.
- **Atmosphere**: Los Angeles smog filtered through golden hour light. The contrast between high-end Beverly Hills luxury and the gritty shadows of the underground club scene, with paparazzi-flash tension around Angel&Co and Night/Model styling.
- **Key Locations**:
  - *Douglas Estate*: Pristine, sterile luxury, highly surveilled, imposing gates.
  - *The Verve*: Dimly lit, neon-accented, grease-stained, cozy but rugged.
  - *Angel&Co*: Boutique fashion/talent studio with severe couture, designer trenches, Saint Laurent sunglasses, and sunflower-yellow off-duty accents.
